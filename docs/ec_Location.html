Class 'Location [ec:Location]'

dcterms:description
A location related to the media resource, e.g. depicted in the resource (possibly fictional) or where the resource was created (shooting location), e.g. A «Location» is used to define the spatial coverage of the story or recording locations like studios or in the field, associated with the EditorialObjects (or Part of EditorialObjects). Note: a type of location shall be defined as a sub-class of Location.
