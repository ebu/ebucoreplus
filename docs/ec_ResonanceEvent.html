Class 'Resonance event [ec:ResonanceEvent]'

dcterms:description
A countable or noticeable consumption-related action by a consumer during or after a consumption, e.g. a click on "play" or on "pause", likes, comments, votes, tweets, preferences, downloads. Resonance events are linked by content consumption. They represent raw data that need to be aggregated (e.g., summed).
