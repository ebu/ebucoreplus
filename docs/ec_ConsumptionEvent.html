Class 'Consumption event [ec:ConsumptionEvent]'

dcterms:description
A location and time span during which consumers of a media service use a device to read, watch or listen to some content that was published within that media service. E.g. a woman watching the latest video of BBC News on YouTube on her smartphone or a family watching a skiing competition during the winter Olympics live on their big screen at home.
