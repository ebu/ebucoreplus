Class 'Campaign [ec:Campaign]'

dcterms:description
A Campaign defines a strategy for publishing content (EditorialObjects and related Resources/MediaResources/Essences) to a targeted Audience according to a PublicationPlan. Campaign strategies can be adapted taking into account the analysis of the Resonance of related ConsumptionEvents (e.g. likes, downloads, etc.). Campaigns can apply to a variety of broadcasting strategies e.g. advertising campaigns, promotional campaigns or else.
