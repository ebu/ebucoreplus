Class 'Consumption licence [ec:ConsumptionLicence]'

dcterms:description
A permit attributed to a consumer defines the terms of content consumption within a media service. E.g., the licence is verified by a mechanism often located in the device, referred to as DRM (Digital Rights Management). Age-restricted content can be consumed only after the consumer verifies their age.
