Class 'Publication service'

dcterms:description
A named media offering provided by a publisher, distributed by a platform and made accessible through a channel for devices to allow people to consume the service, e.g. a linear audio service distributed via FM; a non-linear video streaming service distributed via a PSM's own VoD platform; a YouTube channel distributed via the YouTube platform.
